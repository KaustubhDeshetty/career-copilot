Perception Engineer, Autonomy
Verity Autonomous Systems · Boulder, CO (Onsite)

Verity builds autonomous inspection drones for utility infrastructure. Our aircraft fly beyond visual line of sight over transmission corridors and must navigate without GPS in some environments.

The Perception team owns how the aircraft understands where it is and what is around it.

You will
- Develop and improve our visual-inertial odometry stack, including front-end feature tracking and back-end optimization
- Work on sensor fusion across camera, IMU, LiDAR, and barometric altimetry, including calibration and time synchronization
- Improve obstacle detection and avoidance in cluttered environments — conductor wires are our hardest case and remain unsolved at range
- Build and maintain the simulation and replay tooling used to regression-test perception changes against logged flights
- Analyze flight logs after anomalies and determine whether perception was the cause
- Support flight test campaigns in the field, including early-morning launches

Requirements
- MS or PhD in robotics, computer vision, or a related field, or equivalent experience
- Strong C++. Our flight-side perception code is C++17 and performance-constrained
- Solid grounding in 3D geometry, state estimation, and optimization — you are comfortable with factor graphs, and you know why a Kalman filter diverges
- Experience with ROS or a comparable robotics middleware
- Comfort with hardware: you will be on the flight line

Nice to have: FAA Part 107, experience with embedded GPU targets, prior work on certification evidence for aviation software.
