Principal AI Architect
Atlas Cloud Partners · Denver, CO (Remote, travel 25%)

Atlas is an AWS Premier Partner. We help enterprise customers land AI workloads on AWS, from first architecture through production.

The role sits between our sales organization and our delivery teams.

Responsibilities
- Lead architecture engagements with enterprise customers: assess the current state, design the target AWS architecture, and produce a migration or build plan the customer's own teams can execute
- Author reference architectures and well-architected reviews for AI workloads on AWS — Bedrock, SageMaker, and the surrounding data and security services
- Support pre-sales as the senior technical voice: scoping calls, proposal defense, and the occasional rescue of a deal that has gone technical
- Build reusable delivery assets — patterns, accelerators, and opinionated defaults our consultants apply across engagements
- Advise customers on the decisions that are expensive to reverse: data residency, account topology, model hosting strategy, cost structure
- Mentor solution architects across the practice

Requirements
- 10+ years, with 5+ designing on AWS at enterprise scale
- Deep AWS AI and data services knowledge; AWS Solutions Architect Professional and a machine learning specialty certification expected
- Proven customer-facing consulting experience at the executive level
- Excellent written communication — the architecture document is the product
- Willingness to travel to customer sites

Hands-on implementation is roughly 20% of this role. If you want to be writing production code most days, this is not it, and we will tell you that in the first interview rather than the fourth.
