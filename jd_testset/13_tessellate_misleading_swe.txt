Software Engineer, Machine Learning
Tessellate Robotics · Pittsburgh, PA

Tessellate builds warehouse automation. Our robots use perception models trained by our Autonomy team; this role is not on that team.

You will join Deployment Engineering, which is responsible for getting models from the Autonomy team onto 3,000 robots in customer warehouses and keeping them working.

The work
- Own the model release pipeline: a perception model is approved by Autonomy and must reach a fleet segment safely, with staged rollout and one-command rollback
- Build the on-robot model monitoring that reports inference latency, confidence distribution shift, and disagreement with a shadow model
- Package and optimize models for the robot's embedded compute — TensorRT conversion, quantization, and validating that the optimized model matches the original within tolerance
- Maintain the model registry and the provenance record. When a robot misbehaves in a customer facility we must be able to say exactly which model version it was running and what data trained it
- Carry the pager for fleet model incidents

You should have
- 4+ years software engineering, ideally with embedded or edge deployment exposure
- Experience with model packaging and optimization toolchains
- Strong CI/CD and release engineering instincts
- C++ or Python; we use both
- Calm judgement during incidents involving physical machines in someone else's warehouse

You will not be designing or training perception models. You will be the reason they work in the field.
