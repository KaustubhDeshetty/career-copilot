Position Summary
The Senior AI/ML Engineer II is responsible for supporting the operationalization of machine learning capabilities across the enterprise in alignment with organizational objectives and applicable regulatory requirements.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities
- Designs, implements, and maintains automated pipelines for the deployment, versioning, and retirement of machine learning models across development, validation, and production environments
- Establishes and maintains model monitoring capabilities including but not limited to data drift detection, performance degradation alerting, and fairness metric surveillance in accordance with our Responsible AI standard
- Maintains the enterprise model inventory and supporting documentation required for internal audit and external regulatory examination
- Implements continuous integration and continuous deployment practices for machine learning assets, including automated validation gates prior to production promotion
- Collaborates with Data Science, Clinical Informatics, Information Security, and Compliance stakeholders
- Participates in a shared on-call rotation supporting production inference services
- Performs other duties as assigned

Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
- Five (5) years of relevant experience, including three (3) years supporting production machine learning systems
- Demonstrated proficiency with containerization and orchestration technologies
- Demonstrated proficiency with CI/CD tooling and infrastructure as code
- Experience with model lifecycle management tooling

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience in a healthcare or other regulated environment
- Familiarity with HIPAA and HITRUST control frameworks
