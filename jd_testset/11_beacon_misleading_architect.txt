AI Architect
Beacon Media Group · Los Angeles, CA

Beacon is a streaming and publishing company. We are hiring an AI Architect for our Content Intelligence group.

Despite the title this is a hands-on engineering role. You will be the most senior individual contributor on a five-person team and you will spend most of your week writing code.

What you'll build
- The retrieval system behind our editorial research assistant, which lets producers search 40 years of archive footage, transcripts, and rights metadata by description
- Agentic workflows that chain retrieval, summarization, and rights-checking into something an editor can use without a manual
- Our prompt and context management layer — versioned, tested, and not scattered through the codebase in string literals
- Evaluation for generation quality, because "the summary was wrong about who said it" is a real complaint we get
- Fine-tuning where the hosted models fall down on our domain vocabulary

What we need
- 7+ years engineering, with 2+ deep in LLM application work
- You have built a retrieval system that worked on messy real-world data, not a clean benchmark
- Comfortable owning technical direction for a small team without a management layer
- Python, and the practical half of the ML stack

To be explicit, since the title misleads: there is no pre-sales component, you will not be producing architecture documents for other teams to implement, and you will not be managing anyone. You will be building the thing.
