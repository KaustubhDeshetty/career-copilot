Cloud AI Engineer — Azure
Borealis Financial Services · Charlotte, NC (Onsite 4 days)

Borealis is a $90B asset manager. We are two years into consolidating AI workloads onto Azure under a single governed landing zone, and we need engineers who know that platform deeply.

What the job is
- Build and operate AI workloads natively on Azure: Azure Machine Learning workspaces, Azure OpenAI deployments, Azure AI Search for retrieval
- Design within our security boundary — private endpoints, managed identities, customer-managed keys, no public egress. Every service you stand up must pass architecture review
- Own capacity and cost for Azure OpenAI quota across eight business units, including PTU allocation and burndown reporting
- Automate environment provisioning with Bicep and Azure DevOps pipelines
- Partner with Risk and Compliance to evidence controls for model deployments in a regulated environment
- Troubleshoot across the stack — when a workload is slow, you determine whether it is the model, the network path, or the AKS node pool

Required
- 4+ years hands-on with Azure specifically. This is not a role where general cloud experience transfers cleanly; we need someone who knows the quirks of AML pipelines and the AI Search indexing API
- Azure Kubernetes Service in production
- Infrastructure as code, Bicep or Terraform
- Experience operating in a regulated environment with formal change control
- Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Engineer Associate or Azure Solutions Architect Expert strongly preferred

We are Microsoft-first and expect to stay that way. If you are looking to work across AWS and GCP, this role will frustrate you.
